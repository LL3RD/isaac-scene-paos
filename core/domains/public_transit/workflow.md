# Public transit workflow

1. Solve platform/track or concourse topology before repeating columns, doors, gates or benches.
2. Use modular authored geometry for structural columns and glazing, but require real assets for screen-door modules, turnstiles, ticket machines and benches when visible.
3. Preserve passenger circulation, platform edge clearance and repeated-module spacing.
4. Validate operational faces of gates, machines and doors and keep the fixed camera out of columns.
5. Treat full station backgrounds as source evidence only in independent reconstruction mode.
