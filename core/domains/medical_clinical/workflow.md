# Medical clinical workflow

1. Treat the patient bed/table, procedure light and primary equipment as anchors; require verified real assets.
2. Preserve sterile circulation around the patient support and staff access on both long sides.
3. Mount ceiling equipment to the ceiling support and floor equipment to the floor; validate every support contact separately.
4. Verify the operating face of monitors, anesthesia machines and carts from previews before authoring facing relations.
5. Never approximate visible clinical anchors with boxes. Record `ASSET_GAP` and exact Synthesis model IDs when no validated alternative exists.
